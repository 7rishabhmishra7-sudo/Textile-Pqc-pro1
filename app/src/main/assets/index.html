Weaving PQC → Process PQC Excel Generator

1. Upload Excel files

Original blank Process PQC template is built into this app. Its layout and formulas are used automatically.

Source sheet: SHEET-1 / Weaving PQC. Output sheet: Process PQC.

2. Enter process details

Product Weight = Applied Net Quantity. Accessories / Sizing / Filling = 0.

3. Calculation preview

Enter Invoice Quantity and both loss percentages.

4. Generate

Ready. Upload Weaving PQC Excel and enter details. Process PQC template is built in.
Output mapping:
  • B:F and A: TC details and allocated consumed quantity
  • G:I: Applied Net Qty, Accessories=0, Product Weight
  • J: Opening input formula
  • K:L: Product and Lot No.
  • M: Loss% formula same as Weaving PQC
  • N: Buyer, O: Invoice, P: GSM, Q: Applied TC No. (N/A)
  • R: Raw Material Balance formula; S: Manufactured or Used formula
  • Template sheet and U, V, Z are ignored and not changed.
